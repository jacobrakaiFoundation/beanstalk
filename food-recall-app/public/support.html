Help with recalls and alerts.

Start with the original FDA notice when a food-safety decision cannot wait. For app problems, privacy questions, or a record that looks wrong, use the routes below.

Check the official source

Beanstalk is independent and may be delayed or incomplete. FDA and USDA FSIS each publish their own notices, and not every recall has a public announcement. A search with no result does not establish that a product is safe.

Contact the Foundation

Report a Beanstalk bug, accessibility barrier, privacy concern, or source discrepancy through the Foundation's current public contact route.

Open contact page

Include these details

  • Beanstalk version and iOS version;
  • what you searched, tapped, or expected;
  • the recall number, product, and original FDA link for a data issue; and
  • a screenshot with personal information removed, if useful.

Do not send an APNs token, app credential, payment information, or private health information.

Fix a missing alert

  • Confirm the term appears as a full word or phrase in the notice.
  • Check notification permission in iOS Settings.
  • Open Beanstalk and review Watchlist status.
  • Use Remove this device from Beanstalk alerts before creating a fresh registration.

Notifications match published announcements. They are not guaranteed to be immediate or complete.

Saved recalls, deletion, and donations

Saved recalls stay on your iPhone and can be removed from the Saved tab. Open Settings → Notifications → Remove this device from Beanstalk alerts to remove your watch terms, push token, and alert registration from the Foundation service. Read the Beanstalk privacy policy for details.

Every Beanstalk feature is free. The optional Donate action opens the Foundation's donation page in your browser; Stripe handles payment only if you continue. A donation unlocks nothing and is never required for support.
