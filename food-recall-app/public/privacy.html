Privacy Support Open the app

Privacy, in plain language.

No account. No ads. No tracking. Beanstalk is available as a web app, an optional Android sideload wrap, a native iPhone app, and a native Android app. What reaches the Foundation depends on which build you use and, on native Android, whether you turn on watchlist notifications.

Effective September 16, 2026

Android sideload (Capacitor web app)

This debug-signed APK wraps the same Beanstalk web app in a WebView. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or push notification service operated by the Foundation.

  • On your device only: your watchlist terms, display preferences, and a short cache of recent results stay in the app's local storage. Clearing storage or uninstalling deletes them.
  • Searches: browse and search requests go directly to FDA's openFDA service (and related public FDA/USDA endpoints the web app uses). The Foundation does not receive those recall searches.
  • Links you open: original FDA and USDA sources open outside the app, and those sites have their own policies.

Native Android app (Compose)

The native client in this repository is separate from the sideload wrap. It has no accounts, analytics, or advertising.

  • On your device: saved recalls, watchlist terms, display preferences, and cached API responses are stored locally. Clearing storage or uninstalling deletes them.
  • Recall browse and search: your phone requests openFDA enforcement data directly from FDA. Those search queries are not sent to the Foundation for browsing.
  • Watchlist matching in the app: when you add watch terms, the app may download a bounded list of recent FDA recall announcements from a Foundation-operated read API so it can show on-device matches. That request does not include your typed search text from the Recalls tab.
  • Optional push alerts: only when your build includes Firebase Cloud Messaging configuration and you turn alerts on, Beanstalk sends your watch terms, an FCM token for this app on your device, and a random app credential to a Foundation-operated alert service. Until then, watch terms stay on the device only.

iPhone: everything stays on your device

The iPhone app has no alerts or notifications. Recalls you save and the words and phrases on your watchlist are stored locally on your device. Watchlist matching happens on your iPhone against recent FDA announcements the app downloads; the app does not send your watch terms, a device token, or any app credential to the Foundation.

Recent announcements come from a Foundation-operated read API. Those requests contain no watch terms or identifiers. The application service records route names instead of full request URLs and does not retain search terms or client IP addresses. The same logging restriction applies to the production HTTPS gateway.

Earlier test builds of the iPhone app could register a device for alerts. If your iPhone did, updating Beanstalk deletes that server registration and the credential stored on your device the next time the app opens with a network connection.

Your choices and deletion

  • On iPhone, use search, browse notices, save recalls, and build a watchlist with no account and no permission prompt.
  • Remove locally saved recalls from the Saved tab, or open Beanstalk Settings → Data on this iPhone → Clear saved recalls and watch terms to delete both. Deleting the app also deletes them.

Services Beanstalk uses

FDA and openFDA. Browsing, searching, and opening original sources makes network requests to FDA services. FDA receives normal web-request information under its website policies. Beanstalk does not use those requests to build a search-history profile.

Firebase Cloud Messaging. On native Android builds configured for push, Google processes the FCM token and notification delivery after you grant permission and enable alerts.

The Foundation donation page and Stripe, only if you tap Donate. The Foundation's donation page opens outside Beanstalk in your browser. Stripe handles payment only if you choose to continue. No payment details are entered into or received by the app. A donation is optional and unlocks no feature.

What the Foundation does not do

Beanstalk has no advertising SDK and does not sell or rent app data, show targeted ads, share app data with data brokers, or combine it with third-party data to track you across apps or websites.

Safety and scope

Beanstalk uses reasonable safeguards, including encrypted HTTPS connections and, when Android alerts are enabled, an app-specific credential stored in Android encrypted storage. No system is risk-free. This policy covers the Beanstalk web app, Android sideload, native Android, and iPhone apps; websites you choose to open have their own policies.

Beanstalk is an independent project of JACOBRAKAI FOUNDATION. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by FDA. Recall information is not medical advice, and a search with no results does not establish that a product is safe.
