A clearer path to the original record.

A recall record can include several product names, lot codes, distribution details, and dates. Beanstalk brings search, filters, and source details from FDA and USDA FSIS together so readers can investigate a record.

It is designed for people checking products or dietary concerns, and for community workers and researchers helping others navigate public information.

Beanstalk is independent of FDA and USDA FSIS. Historical records may be incomplete or outdated. No result means no match was found; it does not establish that a product is safe. Check the original notice. This is not medical advice.

Proposed funding priorities

Support a specific next step.

These work packages would help us test and improve the service. A grant's scope, cost, timeline, and reporting schedule would be agreed before work is committed.

  1. Make recall search easier to use.

    Test search, finding a lot code, and opening the original source with intended users. Fix observed barriers on mobile, with enlarged text, and with assistive technology.

    Proposed measure: task completion, time to find the source, and errors before and after improvements.

  2. Make source failures understandable.

    Verify how the app handles unavailable sources, saved results, and missing data. Document what each source covers and how recovery works.

    Proposed measure: results from documented failure scenarios, unresolved discrepancies, and recovery time.

  3. Verify the first iPhone release.

    Complete signed-device testing, notification and privacy checks, and the release evidence package. Record App Review and publication separately.

    Proposed measure: recorded checks for closed-app delivery, duplicate suppression, device removal, and offline relaunch.
