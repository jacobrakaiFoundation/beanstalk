## Full description

> Understand what a food recall announcement actually says.
>
> Beanstalk is a calm, searchable way to browse food recall announcements published by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration. Save important notices on your Android device, create a watchlist for products or hazards you care about, and choose whether to receive matching notifications.
>
> FEATURES
>
> • Browse recent FDA-published food recall announcements
> • Search separately labeled historical food enforcement records from openFDA
> • See product, lot, company, hazard, distribution, and source details
> • Save recalls on your device for offline access
> • Create whole-word and phrase watchlists
> • Open the original FDA source and share a notice
>
> SOURCES AND LIMITS
>
> Recent notices come from FDA's Food Safety Recalls feed and the linked FDA announcements: https://www.fda.gov/about-fda/contact-fda/stay-informed/rss-feeds/food-safety-recalls/rss.xml
>
> Historical search uses the openFDA food enforcement archive: https://open.fda.gov/apis/food/enforcement/
>
> Notifications are a convenience and may be delayed or incomplete. FDA notes that not every recall has a public announcement. A search with no result does not establish that a product is safe. Verify the original FDA notice and follow current instructions from FDA and the recalling firm. Also check USDA FSIS for meat, poultry, and processed egg product recalls.
>
> Beanstalk is an independent public-service project of JACOBRAKAI FOUNDATION. It does not represent, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by, FDA or any government entity.
>
> HEALTH DISCLAIMER
>
> Beanstalk is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
>
> PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
>
> No account is required. Saved recalls stay on your device. If you enable alerts, Beanstalk sends your watch terms, a Firebase Installation ID, and an app-specific random credential to the Foundation's service only to match and deliver recall notifications. There are no ads or cross-app tracking.
>
> Every feature is free. An optional donation link in Settings opens the Foundation's tax-exempt donation page in your browser and unlocks nothing.

## Required Play Console declarations

- **Government apps:** answer that the app is not a government app. The listing and app must retain the visible `.gov` source and non-affiliation statement.
- **Health apps:** declare **Disease Prevention and Public Health** because the app supplies public-health recall information. It is not a medical device and does not access Android health permissions or personal clinical records.
- **Data safety:** use the working answers in [`data-safety.md`](data-safety.md), then audit the signed bundle, server, proxy logs, and current Firebase disclosure before submission.
- **Content rating:** answer the live IARC questionnaire from the submitted build. FDA notice text may contain illness, symptoms, emergency-care, or treatment information; do not understate it.
- **Target audience:** adults and people responsible for household purchases; the visual treatment and listing are not aimed at children.
- **Content rights:** state that the app reproduces public FDA/openFDA facts with attribution and direct source links.
- **Ads and purchases:** no ads, in-app products, subscriptions, or Play Billing permission.
