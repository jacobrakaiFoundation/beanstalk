# Google Play visual-asset plan

Capture the signed Release build with real, current FDA content. Do not use fictional records, notification mockups, an FDA logo, edited status bars, debug controls, placeholder URLs, or personal notification content.

## Required assets

- **Store icon:** [`assets/play-store-icon-512.png`](assets/play-store-icon-512.png) is prepared at 512 × 512 px as a 32-bit PNG with alpha.
- **Feature graphic:** [`assets/feature-graphic-1024x500.jpg`](assets/feature-graphic-1024x500.jpg) is prepared at 1024 × 500 px without an alpha channel. Its editable source is [`assets/feature-graphic.svg`](assets/feature-graphic.svg).
- **Phone screenshots:** provide at least four portrait captures at 1080 × 1920 px or higher for recommendation eligibility. Google requires at least two screenshots overall and permits up to eight per device type.
- Add concise alt text for every image. Verify the current specifications before export.

## Shot order

| # | Screen | Caption | Alt text focus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Recent recall list | `Food recalls, clearly sourced` | Recent FDA recall cards with publication dates |
| 2 | Search and filters | `Find products, hazards, and firms` | Recall search with active filters |
| 3 | Notice detail | `Products, lots, dates, and sources` | Product identifiers and original FDA link |
| 4 | Saved | `Keep important notices on your device` | Locally saved notices and offline-copy warning |
| 5 | Watchlist | `Watch for exact words and phrases` | Watch terms with visible matched phrase evidence |
| 6 | Notification explainer | `Optional alerts, requested in context` | Permission explanation after first watch term |
| 7 | Historical Search | `Search the openFDA enforcement archive` | Separately labeled historical results and dates |
| 8 | Settings | `Privacy controls and clear limits` | Device deletion, privacy, support, and source links |

Use the same approved copy and real records throughout. Inspect every image at 100% for clipping, private data, source accuracy, contrast, and misleading claims.
